## Priority A reproduction and correction

- Target: `/tools/roll-diameter.html`; normal control: Paper Thickness Calculator. Production HTML had unitless length/thickness/core labels and no result unit. Target returned 200, so the issue is arithmetic/input contract, not discovery or deployment.
- The published example specified **100 m length / 0.10 mm caliper / 76 mm core OD**. Chrome reproduced `76.08` from those inputs before repair; an independent annulus calculation is **136.045563865 mm**, displayed **136.05 mm**.
- Root cause: `2 * sqrt(length * thickness / PI + core^2 / 4)` combined metre length with mm dimensions without converting by 1,000. The shape formula is valid only with a common dimensional basis. Defaults 100 / 10 / 10 and generic output further obscured the intended production units.
- Corrected contract: length m, complete wound caliper mm, core OD mm, output mm. Preserve IDs a/b/c/result, Calculate/Reset, URL, title/meta/H1/canonical/JSON-LD/GA4, all CSS, result design, global Copy logic, sitemap, robots, llms and owner badges. Only the `rolldiameter` branch changes; 23 other expansion branches produce byte-identical output against the start-commit source at unchanged fixtures.
- Example defaults 100 / 0.10 / 76; validation rejects blank/zero/negative/non-numeric and overflow inputs. Source-specific guidance covers micron/mm, feet/metres, core bore versus OD, full liner/adhesive caliper, uniform winding and lack of guaranteed machine clearance.
- Formula basis: conserve annulus cross-sectional area, `L_mm * t_mm = PI/4 * (D_mm^2 - core_OD_mm^2)`. The [Avery Dennison converter workflow](https://label.averydennison.com/eu/en/home/customer-tools-na/roll-calculator.html) independently supports the operational inputs; [its product finder](https://products.averydennison.com/) explicitly separates metres, micron/mm thickness and mm OD. PPL uses mm caliper consistently rather than copying a supplier material database.
- Changed production files: target HTML, its scoped branch in `assets/js/calculators/expansion.js`, and the retained generator's Roll Diameter input/example source. No generator was run across the site.
