Roll Fold Panel Calculator

Transparent post-press planning with editable job assumptions.

Enter values to calculate.

What this calculator solves

Plan a progressive roll fold where each folding-in panel may need to be narrower than the panel it nests into. It makes the job assumptions visible instead of treating a generic production rule as a guaranteed specification.

Roll Fold Panel Calculator is a job-ticket planning value: the folding dummy, measured stock and selected equipment remain the controlling evidence.

When to use it and how to choose inputs

Use Roll Fold Panel Calculator after trim size and production sequence are known, but before artwork is released or materials are ordered. Enter values in one unit system and choose the allowance, rate and costs from the actual job ticket; editable example values are not shop standards.

For Roll Fold Panel Calculator, metric and imperial dimensions work when consistently entered. Zero, negative, missing and non-finite values are rejected because they cannot produce a useful production plan.

Calculation method

Starting at the outside panel, each following nested panel is reduced by the user-entered step-down; the base width balances the total flat width.

Roll Fold Panel Calculator shows its arithmetic rather than claiming to certify machine setup. Round sheet, roll and lift counts up only where a whole physical unit is required.

Worked production example

For 17 in, four panels and 1/16 in step-down, the schedule balances to 4.34375, 4.28125, 4.21875 and 4.15625 in.

Record Roll Fold Panel Calculator inputs with the job: a later change in stock, sequence, make-ready or process speed should trigger a fresh estimate.

Interpretation and common mistakes

A roll fold is not a Z fold; equal panels can bind when they must nest.

Compare the Roll Fold Panel Calculator result with the approved job specification and test a dummy where a fold, coating or material behaviour can change the outcome.

Assumptions and limitations

Roll Fold Panel Calculator is an estimating and preflight aid, not a production-certified value. Stock caliper, grain, coating, cure, static, curl, blade condition, fold direction and equipment setup can materially change results.
