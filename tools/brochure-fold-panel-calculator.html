Brochure Fold Panel Calculator

Transparent post-press planning with editable job assumptions.

Enter values to calculate.

What this calculator solves

Plan basic brochure panels without confusing equal-panel Z folds with a roll-fold nesting adjustment. It makes the job assumptions visible instead of treating a generic production rule as a guaranteed specification.

Brochure Fold Panel Calculator is a job-ticket planning value: the folding dummy, measured stock and selected equipment remain the controlling evidence.

When to use it and how to choose inputs

Use Brochure Fold Panel Calculator after trim size and production sequence are known, but before artwork is released or materials are ordered. Enter values in one unit system and choose the allowance, rate and costs from the actual job ticket; editable example values are not shop standards.

For Brochure Fold Panel Calculator, metric and imperial dimensions work when consistently entered. Zero, negative, missing and non-finite values are rejected because they cannot produce a useful production plan.

Calculation method

For a Z fold, each panel is flat width ÷ panel count. For a tri-fold, the closing panel is reduced by the entered tuck and the remainder is shared by the other two panels.

Brochure Fold Panel Calculator shows its arithmetic rather than claiming to certify machine setup. Round sheet, roll and lift counts up only where a whole physical unit is required.

Worked production example

An 11 in tri-fold with a 0.125 in tuck produces 3.6875, 3.6875 and 3.625 in panels.

Record Brochure Fold Panel Calculator inputs with the job: a later change in stock, sequence, make-ready or process speed should trigger a fresh estimate.

Interpretation and common mistakes

Do not use an automatic tuck amount as a stock rule; verify the dummy and fold direction.

Compare the Brochure Fold Panel Calculator result with the approved job specification and test a dummy where a fold, coating or material behaviour can change the outcome.

Assumptions and limitations

Brochure Fold Panel Calculator is an estimating and preflight aid, not a production-certified value. Stock caliper, grain, coating, cure, static, curl, blade condition, fold direction and equipment setup can materially change results.
