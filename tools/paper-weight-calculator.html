Paper Weight Calculator

Calculate the mass of a sheet using metric dimensions and gsm.

Enter values.

Formula: width × height ÷ 1,000,000 × gsm ÷ 1,000. Actual delivered weight may vary by tolerance and moisture.

What this calculator solves

Paper Weight Calculator is used during paper ordering, mailing and handling estimation. It turns a stated job condition into a transparent planning number so an estimator, prepress operator or production coordinator can identify the next decision before committing stock, artwork or press time.

Paper Weight Calculator is a planning tool rather than a production certificate: use its result to document a decision, then retain the source specification with the job.

How to choose the inputs

Use the actual sheet width and height in millimetres and the specified GSM. For a finished product, first decide whether you need the trimmed piece weight or the parent-sheet weight consumed in production.

The fields on this Paper Weight Calculator page are: Width (mm), Height (mm), Paper weight (gsm). Read the label’s unit literally and convert source measurements before entry. A numeric result is meaningful only when every input refers to the same finished piece, press sheet, roll length or production quantity.

How the calculation works

Paper Weight Calculator uses the entered values in the displayed order, preserves the calculation precision internally and rounds the displayed planning value. Where a result represents a count of sheets, jobs or layouts, round up only after deciding which quantity must be whole.

Check the Paper Weight Calculator relationship against the job ticket: the arithmetic can be exact while the selected production basis is wrong.

Worked production example

A 700 by 500 mm sheet at 120 gsm has 0.35 m² area and weighs 42 g. Multiply it by the press-sheet quantity to estimate pallet mass before adding packaging.

Record the Paper Weight Calculator inputs and result on the estimate or production ticket. That lets a later operator trace whether a change came from quantity, stock, layout or a changed process allowance.

How to interpret the result

Paper Weight Calculator provides a planning value, not an automatic release instruction. Apply its result to the relevant quote, material order, artwork brief, press plan, finishing schedule or inventory reservation, then compare it with the approved job specification.

If the Paper Weight Calculator value sits close to a sheet, roll or capacity limit, rerun the calculation with the actual machine and supplier conditions before committing.

Common production mistakes

A frequent Paper Weight Calculator error is entering inches as millimetres, using basis weight in place of GSM, or multiplying by impressions when one sheet carries multiple items. Also confirm that quantity means saleable pieces, sheets, impressions or linear length as labelled; those measures are not interchangeable.

For Paper Weight Calculator, keep spoilage, makeready, overage and finishing loss visible as separate assumptions so they are not counted twice.

Production assumptions and limitations

Mill tolerance, moisture, coating and inserts are outside the sheet-mass formula. Paper Weight Calculator does not certify equipment fit, final colour, binding performance or delivered quality.

Before releasing a Paper Weight Calculator decision, confirm the press, stock, RIP, drying, coating, binding and finishing conditions that apply to this job rather than relying on a universal default.

Related production workflow

Start with the job ticket and approved artwork or stock specification, use this calculation at the paper ordering, mailing and handling estimation stage, then carry the recorded result into the next estimating, prepress or production decision.
